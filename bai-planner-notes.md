# Bai Planner v1

The planner compares several deterministic shopping strategies before applying one.

- Economy: lower spend target, multi-store allowed, staple-heavy basket.
- Balanced: regular mixed basket.
- Easy: minimal cooking and quick food.
- Hearty / Healthy are selected when the conversation asks for those goals.
- The assistant recommends one option using conversation intent and budget fit.
- Strategy cards are previews only; the basket changes only after the user selects a card.
- All totals come from the existing store adapters and shopping optimizer; the planner does not invent prices.
