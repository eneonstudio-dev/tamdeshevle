# VOTONOBAI — Brand Canon

**Status:** owner-approved brand decision; binding for public-brand work  
**Recorded in repo:** 2026-09-16

## Canonical brand

- **Primary Latin brand:** `VOTONOBAI`
- **Canonical Russian rendering:** `Вотонобай`
- **Assistant / character:** `Бай`

`Votonobay` is a superseded spelling and must not be introduced into new public copy.

## «Там Дешевле» relationship

The former product name **«Там дешевле» / «Там Дешевле» / «Тамдешевле» is not the canonical product name**.

It may be used only as ordinary advertising/descriptive language when the phrase makes semantic sense (for example, a sentence about finding where a basket is cheaper). It must not be presented as a second product/brand name, logo, app name, PWA name or primary header.

Do not reintroduce the old `ТД` identity or old «Там дешевле» brand system.

## Product meaning

VOTONOBAI is not limited to “the cheapest option”. The product helps find the **best option for the user's conditions**, which can include price, quality, health preferences, time, convenience and combinations of those factors.

Brand interpretation:

- **«Вот оно»** — the suitable option has been found;
- **BAY / Бай** — the assistant/persona that helps find it.

Positioning: a personal AI buyer / shopping assistant on the user's side, not merely a price-comparison table.

## Public-copy rules

New public copy should follow these rules:

1. Use `VOTONOBAI` for the Latin brand form.
2. Use `Вотонобай` when a Russian rendering is preferable in visible UI.
3. Use `Бай` for the assistant character.
4. Do not use `Votonobay` as a public brand spelling.
5. Do not use «Там Дешевле» / «Тамдешевле» as the product name.
6. Do not imply retailer partnership, official retailer status, ordering capability or verified price scope beyond what the product can prove.
7. Product language should describe “как лучше купить”, not reduce the promise to “самое дешёвое = лучшее”.

## Migration rule

Existing legacy strings are migration debt, not alternate approved brands.

Normalize them only through reviewed changes with regression coverage. Do not perform blind mass replacement where a phrase is descriptive rather than a brand token.

Gate G public-brand consistency is complete only after browser-visible surfaces, metadata/PWA naming and launch copy are verified against this canon on fresh `main`.

## Out of scope

This brand canon does not itself prove:

- trademark clearance;
- domain availability/control;
- social-handle availability/control;
- legal right to use a mark in every jurisdiction.

Those remain separate Gate G evidence requirements.
